Account & Data Deletion Request

At Family Security (developed by Quantum limited), we respect your privacy and provide full control over your personal data.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Family Security app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings > Account.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the action. Your account, profile, paired children data, location history, and safe zones will be permanently deleted immediately.

How to request deletion via email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can submit a deletion request by contacting our support team:

Support Email: sattarzhanovdev@gmail.com

Please include the username or email associated with your parent account. Requests are processed within 48 hours.

What data is deleted

  • Account credentials, usernames, display names, and profile photos.
  • Parent-child pairings and invite codes.
  • All historical GPS location updates, addresses, and safe zone configurations.
  • Family chat messages, tasks, attachments, and alert notifications.
  • Device statistics, app usage logs, and FCM push notification tokens.

Data retention policy

Upon account deletion, all personal data is purged from our primary database immediately and completely removed from backups within 30 days. No user data is retained or sold.
